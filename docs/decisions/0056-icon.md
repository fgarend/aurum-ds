# 0056. Icon

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-11-02

## Context

Lucide is Aurum's icon family (decision 0029), and how the package depends on
it, the stroke and the names were left to the `Icon` component. The app
already has an `Icon` that maps its own concepts (a note, a movement, an
account) to Lucide drawings, most of them of its domain. The original design
draws its icons on a 24 px grid with a 1.5 stroke, at 16, 18, 20 or 22 px,
hidden from screen readers unless they carry meaning alone.

## Decision

- **`Icon` takes the drawing**: `<Icon icon={Wallet} />`. The app keeps its
  map of concepts and draws through Aurum's `Icon`; the design system knows no
  concept of the app.
- **`lucide-react` is a peer dependency**, `^1.61.0`, so the app's drawings
  and Aurum's own come from one copy; the exact version used here is a
  development dependency.
- **The stroke is 1.5, fixed**, the original design's.
- **`size` is in pixels, 20 by default.**
- **Hidden from screen readers by default**; with `label`, it is an image
  named by it. The label comes from the app (decision 0054).
- **It takes the colour of the text around it.**

## Alternatives considered

- **Names of concepts in Aurum** (`<Icon name="account" />`). Shorter in the
  app, but the app's domain inside the design system, and a release for each
  new concept.
- **A dependency on Lucide.** Nothing for the app to install, but two copies
  when versions differ.
- **Lucide's 2 stroke, or a stroke prop.** Lucide's default, but not the
  design's, and icons of two weights side by side.
- **Named sizes.** Only agreed sizes, but not the 18 and 22 the design uses,
  and no token behind them.

## Consequences

- The artifact's names that Lucide spells differently (`arrows-swap`, `more`,
  `piggy`) need no mapping: the app passes Lucide's drawings.
- The components that show an icon take a drawing too.
