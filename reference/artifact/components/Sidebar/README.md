# Sidebar

The desktop navigation rail, `sidebar-width` wide, on `surface` with a `line` border.

**Props** — `size`: `md` (248px, 40px items, default) | `lg` (272px, 46px items, 15px text — for people who prefer larger type) · `collapsible` adds a “Collapse menu” control at the bottom; `collapsed` + `onCollapsedChange` or `defaultCollapsed` show the icon-only rail (72px; hovering or focusing an icon shows its name in a graphite label beside the rail; labels stay for screen readers; sections become hairlines, counts hide except `countTone: 'attention'`, which shows a gold dot) · `collapseLabel` / `expandLabel` · `scopePlacement`: `bottom` (default, above the footer) | `top` · `variant`: `surface` (white rail with a `line` border, default) | `canvas` (no panel: the rail sits on the page background and the active item is a white chip) | `inverse` (graphite rail: a quiet `inverse-top` → `inverse-bottom` gradient with a faint gold glow; the active item is a translucent white fill with a gold icon) · `brandMark` (one or two letters in a 28px tile) · `brand` (node: product name or logo) · `items`: `[{id, label, icon, count?, countTone?, href?}]` (with `href` each item is a real link — use it for page navigation) and `{section: 'Label', collapsible?, defaultOpen?}` dividers — a collapsible section (use it for a long Settings group at the end) shows a chevron and hides its items when closed; open it by default on its own pages · `scopes`: `[{id, label, initials?, tone?, people?, href?}]` — a “Viewing” switch under the brand that chooses whose data the app shows (one person or the household); `scope` + `onScopeChange(id)` or `defaultScope`; `scopeLabel` · `active` + `onSelect(id)` or `defaultActive` · `footer` (node: profile, settings) · `label` (aria label).

**Use** — 5–8 top-level items; the active one is `primary` on `primary-soft`. Below `breakpoint-md` swap to BottomNav.

**Choosing a variant** — On a cool `canvas`, `surface` reads cleanly. On a warmer or very light page ground a white rail looks like a stray panel: Aurum' default product look is `inverse`, which anchors the layout in graphite against the light sea-salt canvas; use `canvas` for the quietest result. Keep one variant per product.

**People and household** — With more than one person in the app, pass one scope per person (`initials` + `tone`) and a last one for everyone together (`people: [...]`, label “Household” / “Família”). The scope filters every screen except shared registries (institutions, issuers, assets, categories). Keep each person's `tone` identical everywhere: avatars, owner markers, breakdowns.

**Layout** — Brand at the top, the items in the middle (they scroll if the screen is short), and a bottom block separated by a hairline with the “Viewing” switch, the footer and the collapse control. In the collapsed rail the switch shows only the current choice (its avatars); clicking it opens a small menu beside the rail to pick another person or the household, without expanding the menu. Escape or a click outside closes it. Keep labels to one or two words so `md` never truncates; every item needs an icon so the collapsed rail still reads.
